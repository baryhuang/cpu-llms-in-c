A113X optimization decisions

KEEP · double-precision NEON stem The isolated 1,100-frame A/B/B/A mean fell from 28.806828 to 19.074221 seconds (1.510249×), with checksum 1635322.4827359049 in every run. The gain transferred to full Q4 and Q5 transcription.
REJECT · four-row × four-output GEMM It was up to 14.8% faster in the short kernel benchmark, but full Q5 compact transcription regressed from 255.093171 to 257.359803 seconds (+0.889%). The implementation was removed from the release source.
REJECT · selecting output tile size from an isolated sweep Tile 32 beat tile 64 in one four-layer two-row microbenchmark (4.627 vs 4.777 seconds), but the ordering interacted with row reuse. No tile-only change was promoted without an end-to-end win.

Evidence boundary

What is established

Target:     ThirdReality TRHub-V3 / Amlogic A113X
CPU:        4× Cortex-A53 at 1.416 GHz
Runtime:    C11 + OpenMP + NEON
Threads:    4, performance governor
Memory:     2 GB; zero process swap
Checkpoint: pinned Whisper large-v3-turbo
Evidence:   target results.json + raw device logs

What is not claimed

No real-time claim for A113X large-v3-turbo
No 32-file corpus WER certification on A113X
No cross-model throughput ranking
No transfer of A113X gains to another Arm SoC
No promotion of microbench-only regressions
No model weights or generated images in git

The Jetson Whisper targets use a broader pinned LibriSpeech suite. The A113X optimization gate is deliberately narrower because a 32-file run is disproportionately expensive on this device.
