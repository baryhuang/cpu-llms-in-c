5. Video VAE optimization

The exact 7×15 256-tile graph has now completed N-to-N: 487.273811 s for Video VAE and 2,418.708237 s total.

The full run recomputed 50 conditioner layers, 200 H3 layer/evaluation calls, 3,780 Video VAE blocks and seven Audio VAE upsampling stages. Packed-image cache hits removed network/import work only. Shell wall was 2,484.38 s because preflight SHA-256 checks are outside the 2,418.708237-second model runtime.
