Result: Qwen3.8-27B is architecturally identical to Qwen3.6-27B (verified tensor-by-tensor against the raw checkpoints: same 1,199 language-model tensors, byte-identical config fields, same shard layout in the mlx-community affine-4bit conversions). The port consists of source pins, a compile script with new hashes, and two MTP packers. Every optimization landed on the 3.6 target — half-tile MMA prefill with S64/S32/S16/S8/S4 buckets, FP16 KV cache, adaptive multi-step speculative decoding, conversation continuation, per-request sampling — applies to these images unchanged.

4. Template semantics

The serving layer implements the 3.8 template: per-request reasoning_effort (low/medium/xhigh, none to disable) injects the verbatim effort instructions at the top of the system turn, thinking replies open at <think> and stream the think block as reasoning_content, and prior turns replay their reasoning (preserve_thinking). A low-effort request that exhausted a 400-token budget mid-think returned the truncated thinking with empty content and finish_reason: length — the reasoning-model convention; give thinking mode a larger budget.
